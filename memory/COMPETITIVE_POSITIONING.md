## Three attack lines
1. **"Yuka counts additives. We catch ultra-processing."** — Yuka has no NOVA/UPF classification; UPF is the hottest US nutrition topic (MAHA/FDA discourse). YAWYE owns it.
2. **Yuka punishes whole foods** (cheese, nuts, almond butter marked down for fat/calories — widely criticized by dietitians). YAWYE's AI reasons "high fat but minimally processed = fine". Perfect side-by-side reel content.
3. **Cheapest subscription in category** (~$2.50/mo vs Yuka up to ~$20/mo, Fooducate $5/mo). US users are 5× more likely to pay for these apps than Europeans.

## Marketing plays
- Influencer briefs: side-by-side comparison reels (YAWYE vs Yuka, same 5 products: whole foods + one UPF item)
- App Store: add "ultra-processed" to subtitle/keywords — Yuka can't claim it
- Position as **the UPF app**, reinforced by the book franchise. Out-niche, don't out-shout.
